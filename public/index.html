AI Voice Appointment Agent

Talk to the receptionist: ask about services, describe what you need, and book a meeting that lands on Google Calendar in real time.

Things to try
  • "What services do you offer, and how much does a voice agent cost?"
  • "I run a dental clinic and want an AI to answer calls. Can I see a demo?"
  • "I need a technical consultation about scaling my app, next Tuesday at 3 PM."
  • Ask for a Sunday or 10 PM. It will suggest open times instead.
  • Spell your email to receive the real invite with a Google Meet link.
